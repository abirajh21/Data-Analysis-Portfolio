# **E-commerce Sales Dataset — Excel and Tableau Project**

This project analyses an e-commerce sales dataset using Excel and Tableau. 
It focuses on identifying revenue trends, regional performance, and customer demographic patterns to drive business profitability and operational efficiency.

---


## **Dataset**

The dataset includes:
- Order details (Order ID, Product ID, Quantity)
- Financial metrics (Price, Discount, Total Amount, Shipping Cost)
- Profitability data (Profit Margin, Profit Status)
- Customer demographics (Age, Age Group, Gender)
- Temporal data (Order Date, Delivery Time)
- Geographical data (Region)

## **Data Cleaning & Transformation (Excel)**

The process involved:
- Standardising order_date formats for time-series analysis
- Calculating Total Amount and Profit Margin per transaction
- Categorising customers into Age Groups (Young Adult, Adult, Middle Age, Old)
- Assigning Profit Status (Profit vs. Loss) to highlight underperforming orders
- Cleaning and validating categorical fields like Region and Category
- Ensuring data consistency for seamless integration into Tableau
---


## **Tableau Dashboard**

The Tableau dashboard visualises:

- KPI Cards (Row 1)
  - Total Revenue ($5.87M)
  - Total Profit ($970K)
  - Total Orders (34.5K)
- Performance & Geospatial (Row 2)
  - Monthly Sales Trend: A line chart showing revenue growth from 2023 to 2025.
  - Sales by Region: A map visualising profit margins across North, South, East, West, and Central regions.
- Product & Customer Deep-Dive (Row 3)
  - Category Performance: A bar chart identifying top revenue drivers like Electronics and Home.
  - Customer Age Group Breakdown: A Pie chart showing the spend contribution of different life stages.
